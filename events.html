🎨 Pixel to Intelligence

📅 Date: TBA
👥 Format: Individual
🎯 Tool: Canva Only
🏫 Open To: All Departments

🎯 Objective

This event aims to enhance students' creativity and improve their understanding of color combinations and design principles — skills that are valuable for future front-end development and design-related work.

📋 Rules

  1. Posters must be created only on Canva during the event
  2. File format: PDF or PNG
  3. Language: English only
  4. Content must be original
  5. Internet allowed only for Canva resources

🚫 Disqualification Conditions

  1. Copied or plagiarized content
  2. Use of AI image generators
  3. Late submission
  4. Violation of any stated rules

⏱️ Event Flow

  1. Registration & Check-in
  2. Briefing of participants (rules, time limit, themes)
  3. Poster Design Round (30–40 minutes on Canva)
  4. Top 6–8 participants shortlisted based on scores
  5. Each shortlisted participant presents their poster (2–3 minutes)
  6. Top 3 finalized based on presentation & design
  7. Result announcement & certificate distribution

📊 Judging Criteria

  1. Creativity & Visual Appeal — 10 points
  2. Relevance to Topic — 10 points
  3. Innovation / Future Thinking — 10 points
  4. Designing Skills — 10 points
  5. Round 2 (Top 6–8): Poster Presentation — 2 to 3 minutes

🧠 Tech Quiz Arena

📅 Date: TBA
👥 Format: Duo (2 Members per Team)
🎯 Focus: Technical Knowledge
🏫 Open To: All Departments

🎯 Objective

This event is fully based on technical knowledge. It helps students identify how much technical knowledge they possess and what knowledge gaps they need to fill.

📋 Rules

  1. 4 teams will be called at a time for the quiz
  2. The top team out of the 4 advances to the next round
  3. Each round consists of 15 questions — 10 MCQs (2 points each) and 5 Non-MCQs (4 points each)
  4. The team that hits the buzzer first gets to answer
  5. If the first team answers incorrectly, the next fastest team gets a chance but receives only half points for MCQs (full points for Non-MCQs)
  6. If the second team also fails — for MCQs, the question is replaced; for Non-MCQs, it passes to the next fastest team
  7. In case of a tie, a tie-breaker round will be conducted with one Non-MCQ — the team that answers first wins
  8. Mobile phones and other electronic devices are strictly prohibited

⏱️ Event Flow

  1. 4 teams called into the lab at a time
  2. Multiple rounds conducted until the top 2 teams remain
  3. Announcement of top 3 & prize distribution

💻 Brain.exe

📅 Date: TBA
👥 Format: Duo (2 Members per Team)
🎯 Type: Guess the Output – C Programming
🏫 Open To: All Departments

🎯 Objective

The objective of this event is to improve students' logical thinking, code analysis, and teamwork skills by predicting the output of given C programs without executing them. A skill-based and competitive event that strengthens problem-solving ability and understanding of core programming concepts.

📋 Rules

  1. Each team must consist of exactly two participants
  2. Only C programming language will be used throughout the event
  3. Use of compiler, mobile phones, or any external assistance is strictly prohibited
  4. Discussion is allowed only between team members
  5. Any form of unfair means will result in immediate disqualification

⏱️ Event Flow

  1. Briefing of participants and rules explanation
  2. Round 1 — Basic Round (Simple C programs)
  3. Round 2 — Semi-Final Round (Logic-based & intermediate C programs)
  4. Round 3 — Final Round (Tricky & advanced C programs)
  5. Result announcement & prize distribution

📊 Judging Criteria

  1. Correctness of output
  2. Logical reasoning
  3. Time taken to answer
