How We Work

Event Workflow

  1. Planning: Event Heads draft and plan their events with clear objectives and timelines
  2. Finance Approval: Finance Department reviews and approves the budget
  3. Design: Design Department creates eye-catching creatives and promotional posters
  4. Marketing: Marketing Department launches promotions and outreach campaigns
  5. Technical Setup: Technical Department handles all technical requirements and setup
  6. Coordination: General Management ensures smooth coordination and execution
  7. Oversight: Advisory Department provides guidance throughout the process
